Privacy notice

Google Drive access is used for the owner's gateway backup and recovery.

This notice covers VoyagerHostingInfrastructure, a personal utility operated by Robin Cox, and this informational website.

Data accessed and purpose

The utility uses Google OAuth and the drive.file permission. This permits access to files it creates or that the account holder explicitly makes available to it. Its intended use is limited to its own backup folders, archives, checksums and harmless connection/recovery test files.

It uploads encrypted gateway archives and reads their contents and metadata (such as names, identifiers, sizes and checksums) to verify transfers, list available recovery points and restore backups. It can manage or delete its backup files under the operator's retention decisions. No automatic deletion policy is currently enabled. It does not scan unrelated Drive files.

Storage and security

Sensitive gateway archives are encrypted locally using age before transfer to the operator's personal Google Drive. The private decryption key remains with the operator, separate from the gateway and its cloud backups. Local source snapshots and restored data are stored in protected operator-controlled locations. Transfers use encrypted connections.

OAuth client details and access/refresh tokens are stored in restricted configuration on the backup host to permit authorised transfers. Operational records may include filenames, timestamps, checksums and error messages. Credentials and private recovery keys are not published on this website.

Sharing and use restrictions

Google processes stored files and metadata as the chosen storage provider. The operator and people explicitly authorised for recovery may handle backups for that purpose. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. No other routine sharing is part of this utility.

VoyagerHostingInfrastructure's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and withdrawal

The operator retains backups for recovery and manages retention manually until a retention policy is adopted. The account holder can delete cloud copies through Google Drive and its Trash, and separately remove local copies. Removing one copy does not remove copies held elsewhere.

Google access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revocation stops authorised future access but does not itself delete existing backups. Losing the private recovery key prevents decryption of encrypted archives.

This website

This static site has no sign-in, forms, advertising, analytics scripts or site-set cookies. GitHub Pages hosts it and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Linked services have their own privacy practices.

Contact and changes

For general questions, contact Robin Cox through the website repository's issues. Issues are public: do not post credentials, keys, backup contents or private account details. Ask for a private channel if your enquiry requires one.

Material changes to data use will be reflected here and communicated to the account holder before the new use; additional consent will be sought where required.
